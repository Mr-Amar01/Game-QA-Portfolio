# TC-RDR2-001 – Verify Mission Objective Progression

## Module
Mission System

## Priority
High

## Type
Functional

## Objective
Verify that completing a mission objective correctly advances the mission to the next objective.

## Preconditions
- Valid mission save or test scenario.
- Mission is active.

## Steps
1. Load the mission scenario.
2. Reach the required objective.
3. Perform the required action.
4. Observe the mission tracker and world marker.

## Expected Result
The completed objective is marked complete and the next objective becomes active.

## Pass Criteria
Mission progression continues without requiring a reload or workaround.
