# TC-RDR2-002 – Verify Horse Mount and Dismount Interactions

## Module
Horse / Player Interaction

## Priority
High

## Type
Functional

## Objective
Verify that the player can reliably mount and dismount the horse when the interaction prompt is available.

## Preconditions
- Player has access to a horse.
- Horse is stationary and reachable.

## Steps
1. Approach the horse.
2. Confirm the mount prompt appears.
3. Mount the horse.
4. Move a short distance.
5. Dismount.
6. Repeat from different approach angles.

## Expected Result
Mount and dismount interactions trigger correctly and player control transitions cleanly.

## Pass Criteria
No missing prompt, failed interaction or incorrect player state occurs.
