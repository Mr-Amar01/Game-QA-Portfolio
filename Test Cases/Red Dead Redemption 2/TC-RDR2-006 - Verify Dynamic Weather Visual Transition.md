# TC-RDR2-006 – Verify Dynamic Weather Visual Transition

## Module
Graphics / Weather

## Priority
Low

## Type
Visual / Compatibility

## Objective
Verify that weather transitions update environmental lighting and effects smoothly.

## Preconditions
- Dynamic weather is enabled.
- Player is outdoors.

## Steps
1. Enter an outdoor environment.
2. Observe the current weather and lighting.
3. Allow a weather transition to occur.
4. Observe lighting, sky, fog and environmental effects.
5. Repeat at different times of day.

## Expected Result
Weather effects and lighting transition smoothly into the new state.

## Pass Criteria
No visible lighting pop, missing effect or persistent incorrect weather state occurs.
