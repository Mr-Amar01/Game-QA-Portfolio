# TC-FH5-002 – Verify Vehicle Respawn Behavior

## Module
Vehicle Recovery

## Type
Functional / Boundary

## Priority
High

## Objective
Verify that the vehicle is safely restored to an appropriate drivable position after leaving the intended playable area.

## Preconditions
- Vehicle recovery or respawn is available.

## Steps
1. Start a race or free-roam drive.
2. Leave the intended drivable area.
3. Trigger vehicle recovery.
4. Observe vehicle position and orientation.
5. Resume driving.

## Expected Result
Vehicle returns to a safe, valid position and can continue driving without unintended advantage or obstruction.

## Pass Criteria
Vehicle recovery is consistent across repeated attempts.
