# TC-FH5-004 – Verify Vehicle Audio Transition

## Module
Vehicle Audio

## Type
Functional / Audio

## Priority
Medium

## Objective
Verify that vehicle audio transitions correctly when switching between different vehicles.

## Preconditions
- Multiple vehicles available.
- Audio output enabled.

## Steps
1. Enter vehicle A.
2. Drive and observe its engine audio.
3. Switch to vehicle B.
4. Return to gameplay.
5. Observe engine, exhaust and relevant vehicle audio.

## Expected Result
Vehicle A audio stops and vehicle B audio starts with the correct audio profile.

## Pass Criteria
No unintended audio overlap or stale vehicle audio remains.
