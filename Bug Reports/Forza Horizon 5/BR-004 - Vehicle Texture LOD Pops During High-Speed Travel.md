# BR-004 – Vehicle Texture LOD Pops During High-Speed Travel

> Portfolio example for QA demonstration.

## Game
Forza Horizon 5

## Platform
PC

## Category
Graphics / Streaming

## Severity
Low

## Priority
Medium

## Frequency
4/5

## Description
Vehicle exterior detail can visibly switch between levels of detail during rapid movement, producing a noticeable visual pop.

## Preconditions
- High-speed vehicle available.
- Camera positioned to clearly observe vehicle detail.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Select a detailed vehicle.
2. Drive at high speed through the open world.
3. Rotate the camera around the vehicle.
4. Observe exterior geometry and textures during rapid movement.

## Expected Result
LOD transitions occur smoothly without distracting visual popping.

## Actual Result
Visible LOD transitions can occur during high-speed travel.

## Impact
Visual quality is reduced during normal gameplay.

## Reproduction Rate
4/5
